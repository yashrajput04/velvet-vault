# Velvet Vault

Design and build a visually stunning, premium e-commerce website homepage with a bold, luxury aesthetic — think high-end fashion/lifestyle brand energy, not a generic template. Use a dark, sophisticated color palette (e.g., black/charcoal background with a rich accent color like gold, emerald, or crimson), elegant typography (modern serif or clean sans-serif for headings), generous whitespace, and smooth micro-animations on hover and scroll.

Header (sticky, glassmorphism/blur effect on scroll):

Bold logo/site name on the left

Center or right-aligned nav links: "About", "Services"

A standout "Login" button (pill-shaped or outlined with a glow/hover animation) on the far right

Hero Section:

Full-width, full-screen hero with a striking background image or gradient overlay

A powerful tagline (e.g., "Redefine Your Everyday Luxury") with a subheading

A prominent "Shop Now" CTA button with a hover animation

Body — "Shop by Category" section:

A grid of 4 premium product cards, each with a high-quality image, subtle zoom-on-hover effect, soft shadow, and rounded corners:

Watches — elegant product image, title "Watches", short tagline, "Explore Collection" button

Bikes — dynamic product image, title "Bikes", short tagline, "Explore Collection" button

Perfumes — luxury bottle image, title "Perfumes", short tagline, "Explore Collection" button

Clothes — stylish fashion image, title "Clothes", short tagline, "Explore Collection" button

Responsive layout: 4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile

Add a subtle fade-in/slide-up animation as each card enters the viewport

Optional trust-building section (between body and footer):

A thin strip with icons/text for "Free Shipping", "Secure Payment", "24/7 Support", "Easy Returns"

Footer:

Clean, dark footer with 3-4 columns: brand tagline, quick links, "Terms & Conditions" link, and social media icons (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn) with hover glow effect

Copyright text at the bottom

Overall feel: premium, cinematic, and modern — similar to a high-end fashion or lifestyle e-commerce brand. Prioritize smooth transitions, polished spacing, and a cohesive color/typography system throughout.

Tips for best results:

Paste this as your very first prompt in a new Lovable project — don't build it up piece by piece, since the AI does better generating a cohesive design in one go.

After the first generation, refine with specific follow-ups like "make the hero image full-bleed with a dark gradient overlay" or "add a subtle parallax effect on scroll."

Ask it to use real stock images: "use high-quality royalty-free images for watches, bikes, perfumes, and clothing" — this alone makes a big visual difference.

For real login functionality (not just a button), follow up with: "add Supabase email/password authentication for the login button."
